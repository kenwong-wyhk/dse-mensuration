The pattern: Paper 1 is almost always one solid changed into another (recast, poured, cut by a plane) and ends with a yes/no part: a claim, "similar?" or "overflow?". The area-ratio MC (shape E) was in 14 of the 15 papers.

2012 also had Q11(b) (2 marks): similar cans, volume ×8 so area ×4. 2013 P2 Q12 is a map scale.

2 · The formula kit

None of this is printed in the paper.

The one rule. Similar figures with linear ratio k: lengths \times k, areas \times k^2, volumes \times k^3. Always go through k: square-root an area ratio, cube-root a volume ratio, then raise to the power you need.

MC traps. The wrong options are the classic slips: the arc alone for the perimeter, the height for the slant height, k^2 for k^3, the small cone for the frustum, the minor segment for the major, and a forgotten \tfrac13.

5 · The last part: claims, "similar?" and "overflow?"

  1. Get both numbers you must compare, each from a line of working; keep \pi until the end. 1M
  2. Compare in the same units (1\text{ m}^3 = 10^6\text{ cm}^3) and write the inequality. 1M
  3. Conclude in words. "Thus, the claim is disagreed." / "Thus, the water will overflow." 1A f.t.

The similarity test, written the DSE way

"\left(\dfrac{\text{height of } X}{\text{height of } Z}\right)^2 = \left(\dfrac63\right)^2 = 4 and \dfrac{\text{TSA of } X}{\text{TSA of } Z} = \dfrac{8640}{960} = 9. Since 9 \ne 4, X and Z are not similar." 2024 P1 Q13(b)

Equal ratios do not prove similarity on their own. In all four recent "similar?" parts the answer was no, shown by one pair of ratios that disagree.

7 · Traps that cost marks

  • Area ratio straight into volume. Areas 4 : 9 give volumes 8 : 27, not 16 : 81 (2017, 2022).
  • The frustum's own height in k. Measure from the vertex: \tfrac{18}{24} (2024), \tfrac{64}{96} (2014).
  • 15 times, so 16 parts (2023).
  • Slant height, not height, in \pi r\ell and on a pyramid's faces.
  • "Total" means every face: both circles of a frustum (2025), the flat face of a hemisphere, the base of a pyramid.
  • Perimeter of a sector is 2r + \ell (2015, 2021, 2025, 2026).
  • Units. \text{cm}^3 \to \text{m}^3 is \div 10^6 (2012, 2014); a map scale 1 : 500 makes areas 1 : 250\,000 (2013).
  • No comparison, no conclusion, no 1A.

8 · MC drill

Endless questions of 41 types, each a twin of a 2012–2026 DSE question with new numbers every time; the tag names the paper it copies. Aim for 75 seconds each, and a streak of 10.

9 · In the exam

  • Time. About 75 seconds per MC; about one minute per mark in Paper 1.
  • Keep \pi and exact values through every line; round only in the final comparison.
  • Draw the cross-section of a cone, frustum or vessel: one right-angled triangle with the radius, height and slant height shows every ratio.
  • Follow-through. A wrong volume in (a) still earns the method marks and the 1A f.t. later. Always finish the comparison.
