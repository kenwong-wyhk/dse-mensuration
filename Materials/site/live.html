Measure every cone from the vertex.

6 | The last part

Two numbers, one comparison, one sentence.

Space left  \tfrac13\pi(20^2)(60) - 4096\pi = 3904\pi\text{ cm}^31M Sphere  \tfrac43\pi(14^3) = \tfrac{10976}{3}\pi \approx 3658.7\pi\text{ cm}^31M Compare  3658.7\pi \lt 3904\pi Conclude  Thus, the water will not overflow.1A f.t.

The same three lines finish every claim: keep \pi, compare in the same units (1\text{ m}^3 = 10^6\text{ cm}^3), then conclude.

7 | Marking scheme

2024 P1 Q13(b)Frustum X: height 6, TSA 8640. Frustum Z: height 3, TSA 960. Are X and Z similar?

\left(\dfrac{\text{height of } X}{\text{height of } Z}\right)^2 = \left(\dfrac63\right)^2 = 4
\dfrac{\text{TSA of } X}{\text{TSA of } Z} = \dfrac{8640}{960} = 9
Since 9 \ne 4, X and Z are not similar.

One pair of ratios that disagree proves "not similar". In all four recent "similar?" parts the answer was no.

6 | Similar?

Six ways to lose a mark you earned.

1

Areas 4 : 9 give volumes 8 : 27. Go through k. (2017, 2022)

2

k is measured from the vertex, not with the frustum's own height. (2014, 2024)

3

15 times the small cone means the whole is 16 parts. (2023)

4

Slant height in \pi r\ell and on a pyramid's faces, not the height.

5

Total means every face: both circles of a frustum (2025), the flat face of a hemisphere.

6

No comparison and sentence, no 1A. "Thus, the claim is disagreed."
