
% Question 12
\question
The height and the base radius of a solid right circular cone are $36\text{ cm}$ and $15\text{ cm}$ respectively. The circular cone is divided into three parts by two planes which are parallel to its base. The heights of the three parts are equal. Express, in terms of $\pi$,
\begin{parts}
\part the volume of the middle part of the circular cone; \rmarks{3}
\part the curved surface area of the middle part of the circular cone. \rmarks{3}
\end{parts}

\begin{solution}
\begin{parts}
\part The volume of the middle part of the circular cone is
\begin{align*}
&= \frac{1}{3}\pi(15^2)(36)\left(\frac{2^3 - 1^3}{3^3}\right) \quad &\text{(1M + 1M)} \\
&= 700\pi\text{ cm}^3 \quad &\text{(1A)}
\end{align*}
\part The curved surface area of the middle part of the circular cone is
\begin{align*}
&= \pi(15)\left(\sqrt{15^2 + 36^2}\right)\left(\frac{2^2 - 1^2}{3^2}\right) \quad &\text{(1M + 1M)} \\
&= 195\pi\text{ cm}^2 \quad &\text{(1A)}
\end{align*}
\end{parts}
\end{solution}
